# Method review and provisional choices

Reviewed 2026-10-26. Sources below are primary project/code sources. Local copies used for inspection are under `outputs/research_audit/sources`; only the explicitly attributed MIT diffusion modules are vendored.

## Action diffusion and visual history

- **Diffusion Policy**: https://diffusion-policy.cs.columbia.edu/ and https://github.com/real-stanford/diffusion_policy . Inspected `diffusion_unet_image_policy.py`, `conditional_unet1d.py`, convolution/timestep components, and `multi_image_obs_encoder.py` at **5ba07ac6661db573af695b419a7947ecb704690f**. It predicts action chunks using conditional denoising and receding-horizon execution. Its image encoder supports shared or separate camera backbones and GroupNorm. We reuse three MIT modules, with only import-path changes; hashes and license are in `visuotactile/models/vendor`. Its global-conditioning path is used; unused local-conditioning compatibility quirk remains as upstream.
- **robomimic**: inspected https://github.com/ARISE-Initiative/robomimic/blob/master/robomimic/models/obs_nets.py . Its observation-group and recurrent wrappers are useful examples of modality separation and sequence handling. No code copied; mutable master is not a dependency. Our finite-window GRU recomputes from the same masked history during training/inference to make reset semantics easy to verify.
- Initial model: shared per-view CNN retaining a 4×4 spatial grid, ordered view concatenation, separate proprio MLP, masked GRU; conditional 1D U-Net (64/128 channels), DDPM epsilon loss and DDIM inference. Images retain 90×160 input resolution. This small CPU-runnable configuration is a starting baseline, not a selected optimum or reproduction of published performance.
- Four observations separated by three control ticks cover 0.15 s. Twelve 29-D actions span 0.2 s; replanning every six native control ticks gives 10 Hz planning. History length/stride, prediction horizon and replan interval are replaceable. No decimation or control gain is changed. Action history is not yet a nominal input; encoder history is. Future causal self-plan history can be added, but teacher/corrected commands must never be substituted.

## Tactile feedback and correction

- **Reactive Diffusion Policy (RSS 2025)**: https://reactive-diffusion-policy.github.io/ and https://github.com/xiaoxiaoxh/reactive_diffusion_policy . Reviewed project description, repository structure, dependency list and `latent_diffusion_unet_image_policy.py` at **824c5e8de1fd1811106907a04b5f0186e0138c0b**. It uses latent action diffusion and a tactile-conditioned asymmetric tokenizer decoder; the code explicitly separates latent prediction from temporal-conditioned decoding. Its slow/fast distinction motivates feedback during chunk execution. We do not copy its robot stack or latent tokenizer: our nominal branch must itself produce native actions. Repo license starts with MIT and includes bundled third-party notices; no files imported.
- **Visuo-Skin (ViSk)**: https://visuoskin.github.io/ and https://github.com/raunaqbhirangi/visuoskin . Inspected `agent/bc.py` at **66519d2ffc09df7df33f1f0807c88f29f849be23**, including shared/separate visual encoders and modality handling. It supports useful low-dimensional tactile learning with magnetic skin hardware; that is not equivalent to PhysX aggregate normal forces. MIT repository, no code copied. Symmetric multimodal fusion would violate our nominal boundary; it remains a possible *correction-only* backbone.
- **OmniTacTune (2026)**: https://colinyu1.github.io/omnitactune-site/ and https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03723 . The authors describe a frozen visual prior, base-policy rollouts for tactile bootstrapping, then online residual RL. Its interface uses predicted base chunks and tactile/context signals, rather than requiring base-network internals. This supports keeping our branches independently replaceable. We reviewed the project method description, not a runnable implementation; no code/license assumed available and no reported success numbers transferred to this project.

## Concrete correction baseline and learning decision

`TactileEncoder` is an MLP over five 3-D force vectors followed by a masked temporal GRU. The signed log1p transform expresses a monotonic compression in Newton units, not calibrated pressure. `CorrectionPolicy` separately owns a visual/proprio history encoder, the tactile encoder, a GRU over a **predicted** nominal future action chunk, and an MLP producing a complete bounded 29-D candidate. It is not an action-difference label generator.

The proposed first training experiment is supervised expert execution conditioned on frozen causal nominal predictions. Teacher actions are labels only; future teacher chunks must not stand in for nominal predictions. Use independent episode splits, optionally cross-fit the nominal predictor, and gather queried expert actions on nominal rollout states to reduce covariate shift. Evaluate tactile shuffle/zero/no-correction ablations. An optional noncontact agreement objective has meaning only with reliable contact validity. Later contact outcome prediction or residual RL can exploit logged transitions; neither objective is frozen.

**No correction training is claimed or enabled.** Current successful teacher trajectories do not alone establish that force improves action choice, and fitting actions may preserve expert preload. The executable path is nominal-only; composition requires an explicitly trained candidate, valid observed contact mask, bounded blend, and native env.step. A boolean gate is an engineering guard, not proof of policy competence or force safety.

The implemented integration probe now adds a separate encoder/previous-issued-command history GRU and masks the unpredicted tail of the current nominal chunk. Online shadow computes on real measurements but records candidates only; CPU replay verifies two contact-rich recorded episodes. These are implementation checks of a proposed architecture, not evidence supporting its learning objective. The subsequent 1,000-update nominal experiment reduced held-out denoising loss to .736 but still achieved 0/2 rollouts with no contact, so improving nominal geometry/visual coverage/data is a prerequisite for interpreting contact correction benefits.

## Still unresolved

No demonstration nominalization, slip estimator, contact pressure estimator, local force calibration, real-world validation, multi-view policy comparison, learned target estimator or correction performance result exists. The fixed task identity is selected externally by choosing a checkpoint; there is no learned multi-task embedding. The fixture is visible in the oblique image, but 160×90 detail and hand occlusion may be insufficient for millimetric insertion. Next camera study should compare an additional calibrated opposing view and higher resolution, with unchanged collisions/physics.
